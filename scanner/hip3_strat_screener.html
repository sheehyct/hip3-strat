Data: served by the scanner worker when hosted (worker-authoritative - analysis and metrics are the exact numbers the alerts fire on, and this browser makes no exchange requests; the status bar shows "data: worker"). Standalone (file://, ?standalone=1, or worker unreachable) falls back to polling the Hyperliquid public info API directly and running detection in-browser. Delisted assets filtered. Category chips use a static map; new listings land in Other until mapped. Daily bars roll 00:00 UTC (20:00 ET). In standalone mode the forming bar is expanded from mids between candle refreshes (monotonic max/min) and trued up against the exchange candle at each bar boundary.

Weekly/Monthly bars are TRUE CALENDAR bars aggregated client-side from dailies - Hyperliquid's native 1w/1M candles are epoch-aligned buckets and would not match standard charts. Momentum: 5m and 1h are exact from the mid tape (5m warms up ~5 min after page load, 1h ~an hour; until then 1h anchors to the 15m close 4 bars back); 15m/30m anchor to 15m candle closes. RVOL = forming 15m volume vs the 20-bar average, pace-adjusted (volume trues up on refetch). Score is a triage heuristic, not a signal (score_v 3: only in-force live signals count - recrossed or invalidated entries contribute nothing - and momentum is normalized by the coin's own average hourly true range, saturating at 2x it). Favorites and layout are stored in this browser only (localStorage).

Progress (mag): 0% at trigger, 100% at target; negative runs toward the STOP on its own scale (-100% = at the stop).
